# 30 Days of Data Science

A 30-day learning challenge alongside my TS Academy Data Science course, shared on LinkedIn with #30DaysOfTech and #LearningWithTS.

**The idea:** every topic gets two days and two problem sets. The first set teaches me the tool. The second set makes me use it on a different problem, so I know I understand it and am not just following along. Each day lives in its own folder with the code, a short write-up, and what went wrong along the way.

## About me

I'm Hadi, a graduate in Industrial Relations and Personnel Management with experience in HR and consumer affairs data. I'm moving into data science and AI/ML engineering, and this repo is a public record of how I'm learning.
